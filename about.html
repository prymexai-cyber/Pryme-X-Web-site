Our Story

A Dual-Continent Engineering Company

Pryme X AI operates across two continents — with engineering and security operations based in Sri Lanka, and client-facing strategy rooted in the United States. That split isn't incidental: it combines deep technical bench strength with direct access to the enterprise clients we serve.

We work at the intersection of three disciplines usually kept separate — artificial intelligence engineering, cyber security, and full-stack product development — because the systems our clients rely on need all three to hold up in production.

Every engagement, from a single landing page to a multi-agent AI deployment, is reviewed by a senior engineer before it reaches a client.
